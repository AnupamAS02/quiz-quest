Brainer basics

Brainer brings AI teammates together to work on a business, with a CEO coordinating the work. This short reference introduces the ideas behind Quiz Quest’s Brainer challenge. Answer five multiple-choice questions when you’re ready.

A team with different jobs

The CEO plans work, assigns it to teammates and checks the results. Specialists handle product building, blog articles, social posts, ads and contacting potential customers. You can ask the CEO to coordinate a request across the team, or talk to a teammate about its own work.

Tasks, routines and shared documents

A task is an assigned piece of work. A routine is a job that runs on a schedule. Teammates use shared company documents to stay on the same plan. To understand what a teammate is doing, look at its work and task status on the Team page.

Explore the official Brainer AI team guide for teammate roles, skills, routines and chat history.

Give direction and follow progress

Tell the CEO the result you want in plain words. It can turn your request into work for the team. When a change needs your approval, the CEO asks and waits for your confirmation.

The roadmap shows the plan in phases. The Dashboard carries a short business update from the CEO, and saved check-ins let you revisit its activity.

See the official Brainer CEO guide for requests, approvals and progress updates.
